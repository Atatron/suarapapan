# SUARA PAPAN — Prompt Produksi Video Kartun

Pakai bersama [Naskah_Video_Kartun_SuaraPapan.md](Naskah_Video_Kartun_SuaraPapan.md).
Tujuan: video kartun 1:58, Bahasa Indonesia, sudut pandang Raka (anak tunarungu).

**Cara pakai (urutan penting):**
1. Tempel **PROMPT 0 (Style Lock)** di awal setiap sesi/chat generator. Ini yang menjaga tokoh tetap sama.
2. Generate **lembar karakter** (PROMPT 1), simpan hasilnya, lampirkan ke semua prompt berikutnya.
3. Generate klip satu per satu pakai **PROMPT 2 (S1–S14)**. Generator video biasanya hanya 5–10 detik per klip, jadi satu prompt panjang untuk 2 menit **tidak** akan konsisten.
4. Tempel klip di CapCut, tambahkan teks, voice-over, dan musik (PROMPT 3).

---

## PROMPT 0 — Style Lock (tempel di awal setiap klip)

```
STYLE LOCK — keep identical across every clip.
Visual style: flat 2D children's storybook animation, soft rounded shapes, thick clean
outlines, gentle paper-cut texture, warm afternoon light, smooth limited-frame animation
(like a modern picture-book short film). 16:9, 1080p, 24 fps.
Setting: an Indonesian public elementary school (SD) classroom. Wooden desks, deep green
chalkboard (#16302A), cream walls (#F3F1EA), window with palm trees, Indonesian map on wall.
Palette: deep chalkboard green #16302A, warm yellow #E8B931, cream #F3F1EA, sage #A8CBB7,
brick-red accents. Gentle, hopeful, never scary.
Characters (must stay identical in every shot):
- RAKA: 7-year-old Indonesian boy, short messy black hair, big round eyes, warm brown skin,
  white shirt + red shorts school uniform, light-blue hearing aid on his RIGHT ear,
  yellow backpack.
- BU SARI: Indonesian female teacher, 30s, olive-green hijab, cream blouse with small flower
  print, long brown skirt, warm smile, small clip-on microphone on her collar (from scene 6 on).
- CLASSMATES: 4-5 children in white-and-red uniforms, one girl with two braids, one boy with glasses.
- RABBIT: simple white cartoon rabbit, upright ears, soft grey inner ears.
Rules: no on-screen text or letters generated by the model (text is added in editing).
No realistic faces, no horror, no dark tones. Camera: slow, simple, steady.
```

## PROMPT 1 — Lembar karakter


## Prompt "satu-lewat" (hanya jika generator mendukung storyboard/multi-shot)

```
Create a 2-minute children's storybook animation in Bahasa Indonesia, 16:9, in the style
described in the STYLE LOCK. Story: "Kelas yang Sunyi". Raka, a 7-year-old deaf boy with a
light-blue hearing aid, cannot follow his teacher's math lesson, until Bu Sari uses
SuaraPapan: her speech appears as large captions and becomes an animated scene on the board
(three rabbits hop in; two leave; Raka is the first to answer). Structure: (1) 15 s hook —
cheerful classroom, then the sound drops and the color fades as we enter Raka's point of
view; (2) 25 s problem — Raka is not alone, a teacher overwhelmed by 30 children;
(3) 55 s solution — captions, rabbits, student answers, how the AI works, teacher control;
(4) 20 s close — low cost, then a callback to the opening shot with Raka's hand raised
first. Mood: gentle, hopeful, warm. No generated on-screen text. Silent clips; dialogue and
text are added in post. End on a deep-green card with space for the SUARA PAPAN logo.
```

> Catatan jujur: prompt satu-lewat jarang menghasilkan tokoh yang konsisten. Pakai hanya untuk draf cepat, lalu ganti dengan klip per adegan di atas.
